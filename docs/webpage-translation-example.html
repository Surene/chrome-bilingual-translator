熵的社会学意义

网页双语对照阅读示例 · 原文保留，译文显示在下方

上周，我读完了《熵：一种新的世界观》。

Last week, I finished reading “Entropy: A New World View.”

作者是两个经济学家，他们写了一本物理学的书！正如经济学家所擅长的，书中充满了夸大偏颇的描写，以及片面的和逻辑不严密的论证。而且这本书（英文版）出版于1980年，内容不是很新。

The authors are two economists who wrote a book on physics. As economists are apt to do, the book is filled with exaggerated and biased descriptions, as well as one-sided and logically loose arguments. Moreover, this book (English edition) was published in 1980, so its content is not very new.

但是，我不得不说，他们的结论说动了我：从熵的角度看，如果不改变发展模式，人类无法继续发展。我还发现，熵是一种解释力极强的理论，许多现象都可以用熵来解释，看上去非常合理。

However, I must say that their conclusion convinced me: from the perspective of entropy, if we do not change our development model, humanity cannot continue to develop. I also found that entropy is a theory with extremely strong explanatory power; many phenomena can be explained by entropy, and it seems very reasonable.

下面我就来解释，什么是熵，以及为什么它会影响人类社会的发展。

Below, I will explain what entropy is and why it affects the development of human society.
